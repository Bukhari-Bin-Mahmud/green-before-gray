green before greyZero Point, Khulna

Methods & sources

Imagery and greenness

Sentinel-2 surface reflectance: matched composites for 15 March–31 May, 2019 and 2026. NDVI compares near-infrared and red reflectance. The 0.35 threshold includes grass, crops and aquatic growth as well as trees. It is not a tree-canopy inventory.

Cloud Score+ ≥ 0.60, scene-classification masks and at least three valid observations per pixel. Optical summaries use exact pixel-overlap weighting in clipped 50 m cells.

Grid references and population

50 m IDs are UTM Zone 45N grid coordinates divided by 50: they locate a grid cell, not a land parcel. The pilot covers 93.42 ha and contains 423 clipped optical cells. Population is a GHSL 2020 model with 100 m source pixels, retained in the original 250 m reporting cells.

Context and illustrations

The boundary is a user-drawn study area near Zero Point. The locality description—western Jhardhanga and eastern Hogladanga—is supplied by the field observer. Satellite change alone does not identify its cause. Temperature and air quality were not assessed. Concept images are AI-assisted illustrations; existing-condition images are field photographs. Memory lane is an imagined illustration based on a resident’s recollection.
